Island Tours

A Journey to Paradise Islands!

Step beyond Bali and explore the unique beauty of its neighboring islands. From secluded exotic beaches and vibrant coral reefs to lively marine life—or even an encounter with the Komodo Dragon—each island offers an unforgettable escape into paradise.

Island Tours

Gili Trawangan Private Snorkeling Tour

Romantic Underwater Statue: This impressive underwater sculpture has become a symbol of the island, perfect for capturing stunning photos. As you swim around it, admire the intricate details of this artwork while being surrounded by vibrant marine life.

‍Turtle Point: A famous spot to swim alongside sea turtles in their natural habitat. You'll have the chance to observe these magnificent creatures gliding gracefully through the crystal-clear waters. Floating nearby, you'll feel a deep connection with nature, enjoying a unique and peaceful experience.

‍Gili Meno: The smallest and most tranquil of the Gili Islands, known for its white sandy beaches, relaxed atmosphere, and clear waters. Here, you can enjoy a light lunch on the beach or unwind before we return to Gili Trawangan. Gili Meno is the perfect place to end the tour with a touch of serenity and natural beauty.

‍Included in the price: Snorkeling equipment, guide, and glass-bottom boat.

Gili Islands
Catamaran Experience

‍Turtle Point: Your snorkeling guides will then lead you through the warm, clear waters to Turtle Point, where you’ll have the chance to swim alongside majestic sea turtles, explore coral reefs, and observe a variety of tropical fish species.

‍Coral Garden (Gili Air): After your exciting snorkeling session, return to the catamaran for a snack. Depending on the weather forecast, we may sail to a third snorkeling spot or continue cruising while the crew serves appetizers for lunch.

‍Water Jumping: While lunch is served, guests can take in the spectacular coastal views of Lombok, including Mount Rinjani. If you haven’t already, grab a drink from the bar and make memories with new and old friends. We’ll also pause in the middle of the ocean to give you the chance to jump directly from the catamaran into the water—an experience we highly recommend!

‍Included in the price: Snacks, water, coffee, full European buffet, premium snorkeling equipment, towel, and GoPro videos.

Island Tours

NUSA PENIDA - WEST TOUR

‍Kelingking Beach: One of the most iconic beaches around Bali. Admire the T-Rex-shaped cliff and enjoy the spectacular scenery of the white sand beach and crystal-clear waters.

‍Broken Beach: Discover an impressive natural arch formed by the erosion of the cliffs. The view here is unique and perfect for capturing unforgettable photos.

‍Angel’s Billabong: Visit this natural pool with crystal-clear waters nestled between rocks. Swim in the warm, clear waters or simply relax and take in the beauty of this idyllic spot.

‍Crystal Bay Beach: Known for its turquoise waters and exotic scenery, this beach is perfect for relaxing on the white sand with a refreshing drink while enjoying stunning views of the bay.

Island Tours

NUSA PENIDA - East TOUR

Diamond Beach: A hidden beach with white sand and crystal-clear waters, surrounded by towering cliffs. It’s the perfect spot to relax and admire the stunning natural beauty.

‍Atuh Beach: Known for its spectacular landscapes and wild natural setting, this beach offers fine sand and turquoise waters, ideal for swimming and unwinding.

‍Rumah Pohon (Tree House): Also called the "Tree House," this panoramic platform built atop a tree offers a unique view of the island’s landscape and the ocean—perfect for soaking in the scenery.

‍Raja Lima: Also known as the "Five Kings," this viewpoint offers breathtaking views of the surrounding cliffs and beaches, making it an ideal spot for spectacular photos.

‍Bukit Teletubbies: A rolling green landscape resembling the setting of the Teletubbies cartoon. Recharge your energy and enjoy the natural beauty of this serene location.
